Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Nattevakt mobile application ("Application").

Nattevakt is developed by JustAnotherAIApp.
Contact: justanotheraiapp@gmail.com

Nattevakt is a freemium neighborhood safety app for Norway. The Application is provided "as is". We do not require an account to use the Application.

Information We Process

Depending on how you use the Application, we may process:

  • Approximate location — to show nearby incidents, define your watch zone ("sone"), and send relevant alerts
  • Watch zone settings — center, radius, and related preferences stored on your device
  • Device and app data — device type, operating system, language, and basic usage/diagnostics needed to run the app
  • Push notification token — to deliver incident alerts
  • Purchase / subscription data — if you buy Nattevakt Premium (handled by Apple and RevenueCat)
  • Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data — if you allow personalized ads (see Advertising)
  • IP address — as typically processed by our hosting and third-party services

Location Data

Location is used only for core features: the map, nearby incidents, zone alerts, and related notifications.

Your zone settings are stored locally on your device. We do not use your location to build a long-term movement profile. Exact practices of third-party SDKs are described in their own policies.

You can revoke location permission in system settings; some features will then be limited.

Advertising

The Application shows ads (including native and rewarded ads) via Google AdMob.

In the EEA/UK and similar regions we use Google’s UMP consent flow. On iOS we may also show Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.

  • If you do not consent or do not allow tracking, we may still show non-personalized or limited ads where allowed
  • You can reopen ad privacy choices in the app where required ("Personvernvalg for annonser")

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not receive your full payment card details. Purchase status is used to unlock Premium features and restore purchases.

Push Notifications

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to send alerts related to your zone and app activity. You can disable notifications in system settings or in the app where available.

Incident Data / Backend

Incident content is loaded from our backend infrastructure (including Supabase) to display safety-related events on the map and in feeds. Requests may include area or zone parameters needed to return relevant results.

How We Use Information

Collected data is used to:

  • Provide map, alerts, zone coverage, and Premium features
  • Show ads and measure ad performance
  • Improve stability and performance
  • Comply with law and respond to support requests

Third-Party Services

The Application uses third-party services that may collect or process information. These providers have their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • RevenueCat — subscriptions
  • Apple — In-App Purchases, ATT, and related system services
  • Firebase (Google) — push notifications
  • Supabase — incident / backend API

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

Data may be shared with the services above only as necessary to operate the Application (advertising, purchases, push notifications, and backend).

Data Retention

On-device settings remain until you change them or uninstall the Application. Server and SDK retention follows each provider’s policy and what is needed to provide the service.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (including the EEA and Norway), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal data, and to object or withdraw consent.

  • Uninstalling the Application stops on-device collection going forward
  • For deletion or other requests, contact us at justanotheraiapp@gmail.com
  • For ad choices, use in-app ad privacy options and/or system tracking settings

Children’s Privacy

The Application is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
